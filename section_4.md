RI: From Physical Foundations to Architectural Controllability

Purpose

The first three sections of the Realistic Intelligence (RI) project establish its initial conceptual, mathematical, and physical framework. They form the foundation upon which further development may be built, but do not yet establish the performance of a complete RI system.

The immediate priority is to complete and evaluate the work defined in these foundational sections, distinguish demonstrated results from unresolved hypotheses, and establish what the proposed physical and computational principles can actually achieve.

The Next Architectural Direction

The next stage concerns architectural controllability: defining a computational architecture in which the intended meaning of a human request determines the admissible transformations from the outset.

The long-term concept explores a unified processing chain:

Language → Geometry → Light → Geometry → Language

Its purpose is to investigate whether geometric representation and physical optical processing can form part of a computational architecture that preserves the task-relevant meaning of the original request.

This remains a proposed direction of development. Its implementation, correctness, energy efficiency, and practical advantages must not be assumed before they are demonstrated.

Conditions for Further Development

The development of RI must proceed incrementally, with each subsequent stage building on the results of the preceding completed stage. Further development of new architectural concepts will begin after the foundational tasks have been completed and their results evaluated.

Preliminary computational approaches and plans for experimental procedures for the next stage of RI development have been prepared. Their implementation and validation will depend on the evaluation of the current stage and the definition of the next development objectives.

The preparation of these approaches and plans does not imply that all calculations have been completed, that the experimental procedures have been validated, or that the next stage of development has begun.

Publication of these materials on GitHub is deferred until a funding partner is secured who is willing to participate in the further development of RI. The decision to publish the materials will be made jointly with that partner as part of the collaborative development of the project.
